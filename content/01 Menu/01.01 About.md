## Who Am I?

Welcome to my corner of the internet. This space is a collection of musings from a woman with various interests and obsessions. You might find anything from PKM ramblings to book reviews to snippets of poetic thoughts. I hope something here entertains you, informs you, or at the very least offers some peace.

This is Neith Lives Here. You may have found me on tumblr or instagram or patreon. Or you may be an irl or online friend I accidentally slipped this url to. Either way, I'm happy you're here. This will be my yearlong (September 2026 - September 2026) project of writing and publishing work consistently. My intentions for this website and project is for it to be a practice space. I am working on ending my lifelong relationship with perfectionism and the fear of failure and the process of failing. I know the best way to overcome those trials is to "just do it" but my brain doesn't work like that. Therefore, I've invested money and time into this space. I _can't_ waste it. My only option now is to try and try again and to be bad at something. I need to fall in love with the process of learning through trial and error.

All of that to say... be prepared for some inconsistency as I attempt this human thing. Send encouragement if you have any (neithliveshere@proton.me). Join me? Maybe? If you're up for it?

In case you're curious about this random internet stranger...

Hello! My name is Neith. I am a recent grad in my early 20s. I study poetics, East Asia, and international politics. I'm american (unfortunately). But I'm also queer (asexual), disabled (chronic pain), Black and Indigenous, a reader, a poet, a novice researcher, a big sister, a tree hugger (maybe I'll write a bit about my zero waste aspirations…), a tattoo collector, an avid hobbyist, an aspiring polyglot (and maybe polymath too), and a lover of all things japanese stationery. I greatly admire Hayao Miyazaki, Maya Angelou, and the RnB genre as a whole. I will make a post (or link my personal wiki...?) about my ethical/moral code and other such things if anyone is interested. 

That's all for now! Thank you for reading. If you'd like to introduce yourself, please send an email!! I am hoping to build community this year (hence the website and socials).

Looking forward to getting to know you,

**Neith**
