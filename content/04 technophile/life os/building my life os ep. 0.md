## [notion](https://www.notion.so/):

I am not a notion girlie, but I can't find an app that lets me build databases the way notion does. at least not one I like. I am a productivity app enthusiast so i've tried just about all of them and notion just works the best. [Here](https://neithliveshere.notion.site/be2ffff842a74cd195b2eef040aa4c77?v=c4b626630f804d9282822c3d27952b5c&pvs=4) is the template I use to keep track of my assignments. It is a mix of [StudyWithAra's template](https://shop.beacons.ai/studywithara/b2432bbf-e728-4575-a9aa-68841fb30622?pageViewSource=lib_view&referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fbeacons.ai%2Fstudywithara&show_back_button=true) and [Notion's Assignment Tracker](https://www.notion.so/templates/assignment-tracker-2). The image above is the template I've made available for all of you! There are automations set up (the lightening icon), but I think everything is pretty self explanatory? if you have any questions don't hesitate to reach out.

## [obsidian](https://obsidian.md/):

my one true love. i can wax poetic about why this is just a superior notetaking app, but that will be for a later post. for now, think of obsidian as a wikipedia database for all your notes. it allows for tags and backlinks and search and callouts... its amazing. love it there. uhm. a bit of a learning curve, but again a whole post on obsidian upcoming. will reveal my obsidian notes in that post I'm working on.
## [timepage](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/timepage-by-moleskine-studio/id989178902?platform=iphone):

if you love stationery you might be familiar with Moleskine. they've built this wonder suite of productivity tools and timepage is the calendar app. I use this on my phone! its everything a calendar app should be and more. its super cute and functional and customizable. I use google calendar to create different calendars that i've color coded to correspond to different areas of my life. i use a couple different calendar apps but timepage is my school calendar. it tells me when to leave for class, how to get there, and how long i have to rest/study/eat between my classes. it shows the weather, alerts you of rain, and did i mention its cute?

## [brainscape:](https://www.brainscape.com/)

i use this to learn whatever I am studying. recently it has been vocab and kanji, but I imagine I might add in grammar cards at some point too. if you are familiar with anki, brainscape has a similar system for "learning", meaning you rank the card after seeing the answer, deciding for yourself how well you know the material. I can also download the decks I make to use during my commute so that is super helpful. And it's free (with a subscription upgrade available)!

## **System:**

okay so i'm not quite sure how to explain this in a way that makes sense, but i shall attempt it. the system as it stands has a few steps.

1. move weekly assignments and such from the syllabi to Notion
2. add due dates and deadlines into Calendars
3. move tasks to Things
4. plot study schedule onto Calendars
5. write essays in Google Docs using notes i wrote in Obsidian during class
6. study for Japanese using Brainscape and Anki for memorization
7. practice Kanji and writing in kokuyo campus notebooks
8. write scripts, take quick notes, etc., using Drafts
9. migrate notes from Drafts to Google Docs, Obsidian, or Calendars depending on actionability
10. commence studying using YouTube as background noise

That's all for now! Thanks for reading ♡
