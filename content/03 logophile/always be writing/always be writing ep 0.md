Today I want to introduce a new project. This is Bossy Bruja’s brainchild. You can find her @bossybruja on IG and @richuals on YT. The video where she explains the challenge/program can be found here! But the program is called Always Be Writing. Bossy talks about Ray Bradbury’s push for writers to read. He directs folks to read one poem, one short story, and one essay every night for 1000 nights. And I’m taking on this challenge starting tomorrow morning. Along with that, Bossy is adding writing to the program. She has run a few scripting challenges throughout the year and this is a natural next step. 

With the way the world is going right now, politically and with AI and such, writing and art and documenting the human experience is so important. I’m so over just complaining and I’m working towards actively contributing to solutions. And so, welcome to Always Be Writing. 

My TBR/syllabus will be released and explained tomorrow. The plan is to focus on writers I admire and discovering new scholars and intersections between different types, styles, and eras of writing. My list right now includes Poe, Shakespeare, Audre Lorde, and Maya Angelou. I’m sourcing my scholarly essays from JSTOR, but I’ll also read any essays the above writers have authored. I’ll attempt to produce mini essays, poems, in-depth essays, and maybe a short story or two. The mini essays will be released on this website, longer essays on Patreon, and the small summaries/main ideas will be posted in my weekly [[Newsletter]]. 

Consider joining me?
